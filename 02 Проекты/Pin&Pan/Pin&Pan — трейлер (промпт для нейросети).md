# 🎬 Pin&Pan — продакшн-промпт для трейлера (Runway / Pika / Sora)

Готовый промпт для генерации 60–75-секундного кинематографичного геймплейного трейлера. Из Telegram «избранное» (05.08.2026).

**Goal:** Create a 60–75 second cinematic gameplay trailer for a 2D co-op puzzle-platformer called "Pin&Pan".

## Style & Visual Direction

- Dark minimalist background (charcoal gray/black)
- Neon glow accents (red, blue, white)
- Two glowing yarn characters connected by a bright thread
- Clean, high-contrast, readable silhouettes
- Smooth camera pans + slight zooms
- Emphasis on the THREAD as the main visual element
- Subtle particles, sparks, glow effects when interacting

## Tone

Starts playful → escalates into chaos → ends emotional. Mix of humor (fail moments) + tension + mystery.

## Scene structure

**Scene 1 (0–3s) — HOOK.** Two yarn characters connected by a thread. One pulls → launches the other across a gap → crash/fail. Text: "One thread. Zero coordination." Fast cut, energetic, chaotic.

**Scene 2 (3–8s) — CORE IDEA.** Close-up of the thread stretching between characters. Text: "You are tied together." Slow motion stretch, glowing tension effect.

**Scene 3 (8–30s) — MECHANICS MONTAGE.** Fast cuts (2–3s each): thread tension opens a door / slingshot launch across spikes / wrapping enemy → immobilized / thread slices obstacle (spark effect) / players pull together → snap into position / synchronized jump → extra height / swap positions (twist effect). Overlay words: Pull / Launch / Trap / Cut / Sync / Swap.

**Scene 4 (30–45s) — CHAOS & FUN.** Fail moments: one player falls and drags the other; mistimed jump; accidental sabotage. Characters wobble, overreact, comedic timing. Text: "Friendship not included."

**Scene 5 (45–60s) — WORLD & STORY.** Reveal darker environment: needles, threads, mechanical loom. Silhouette of "The Weaver". Thread tightens between characters. Text: "Break the thread… …or become one." Music becomes emotional.

**Scene 6 (60–75s) — FINAL SHOT.** Both characters at a glowing choice point. Thread shines intensely. Fade to black. Title: "Pin&Pan". Call to action: "Wishlist on Steam".

## Technical specs

Resolution 1920×1080, 16:9, 60 FPS. Camera: dynamic side-scrolling + cinematic zoom. Lighting: neon glow, high contrast. Effects: bloom, particles, sparks on interaction.

## Audio

Start: light playful electronic. Middle: fast chaotic rhythm. End: emotional ambient tone. SFX: elastic stretch sound, snap/release, soft impact/fail sounds.

## Important constraints

- Each mechanic must be instantly understandable visually (no UI)
- Keep scenes short and punchy
- Avoid clutter — focus on thread interactions
- Ensure readability on small screens (Steam thumbnails, mobile)

## Extra

Create 2 versions if the generator supports variations:
1. Gameplay-focused (fast, chaotic)
2. Emotional/story-focused (slower pacing, more atmosphere)

Возможные доработки: адаптировать под конкретный инструмент (например, Runway Gen-3 с точными параметрами) или сделать отдельный промпт для виральных TikTok-клипов.
